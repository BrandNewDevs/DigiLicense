# Delhi permanent driving-licence fee guidance

This reviewed extract is a short, neutral paraphrase of the Delhi public permanent driving-licence
page. It is retained as a fact-packet source, not as a payment flow.

## Permanent driving-licence fee

The reviewed Delhi public permanent driving-licence page lists a revised fee of 400 Indian rupees,
including the smart-card amount shown there. Fees can change, and DigiLicense does not accept
payment.
