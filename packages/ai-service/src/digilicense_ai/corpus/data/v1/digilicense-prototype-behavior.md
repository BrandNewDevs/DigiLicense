# DigiLicense prototype behavior

This is internal, reviewed prototype guidance. It must never be represented as public policy or
as a government action.

## Guided steps and locked actions

DigiLicense explains the current prototype step and why a displayed action is locked. It does not
decide legal eligibility, verify records, or change an application.

## Waitlist and offers

The DigiLicense appointment waitlist and any offer timer are simulated prototype behavior. They
demonstrate a transparent workflow and do not reserve a real appointment.

## Status, payment, address, and mobile updates

DigiLicense status, payment, address-change, and mobile-update flows are synthetic. The assistant
can explain a displayed reason code but cannot inspect or alter personal records.

## Scope and fallback

The assistant provides evidence-grounded explanations only. If reviewed evidence is insufficient,
it returns deterministic guidance instead of guessing.
