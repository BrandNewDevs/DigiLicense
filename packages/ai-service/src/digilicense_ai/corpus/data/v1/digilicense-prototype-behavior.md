# DigiLicense prototype behavior

This is reviewed guidance about the independent DigiLicense prototype. It must never be represented
as public policy or as a government action.

## Learner's licence

The learner's-licence workflow guides a user through the form, recorded submission, fee step, and
next action shown by DigiLicense. It is simulated prototype behavior and does not submit an official
application or decide legal eligibility.

## Learner's test

The learner's-test workflow provides a preparation checklist, an in-product test, and a recorded
result. It is simulated prototype behavior; passing it does not create an official learner licence.

## Permanent driving licence

The permanent-driving-licence workflow explains displayed eligibility dates or locked actions,
records the form and fee steps, and leads to appointment preferences. It is simulated prototype
behavior and neither decides legal eligibility nor submits an official application.

## Renewal

The renewal workflow records its guided form, displayed fee action, and status inside DigiLicense.
It is simulated prototype behavior and does not renew an official driving licence.

## Duplicate or replacement

The duplicate-or-replacement workflow records its guided request and status inside DigiLicense. It
is simulated prototype behavior and does not issue or replace an official driving licence.

## Change of address

The change-of-address workflow records local verification, proof, review, and status steps. It is
simulated prototype behavior and does not alter a government record.

## Mobile-number update

The mobile-update workflow records local verification and status. Its optional mock Aadhaar proof
is simulated prototype behavior; DigiLicense does not contact Aadhaar or change a government record.

## Application status

The application-status workflow explains only persisted synthetic DigiLicense records and their
displayed reason codes. It is simulated prototype behavior and cannot inspect a government system.

## Fees and payment

The fee catalogue and payment result are recorded only inside DigiLicense. Payment is a simulated
workflow: no real money is charged and no government fee is confirmed.

## Appointment waitlist and offers

The appointment workflow records zone and delivery preferences, waitlist membership, offer timers,
and confirmation inside DigiLicense. Appointment reservation is a simulated workflow and does not
reserve a real appointment.

## Assistant scope and fallback

The assistant explains only these ten DigiLicense workflows from reviewed prototype evidence. It
does not provide real-world policy, legal advice, government links, or general knowledge. If the
evidence is insufficient, it returns bounded DigiLicense guidance instead of guessing.
