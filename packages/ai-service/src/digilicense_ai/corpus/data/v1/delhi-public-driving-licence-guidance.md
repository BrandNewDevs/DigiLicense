# Delhi public driving-licence guidance

This reviewed extract is a short, neutral paraphrase for evidence retrieval. It is not a
transactional workflow and does not reproduce forms or collect applicant data.

## Learner licence and preparation

Delhi public guidance describes a learner licence as temporary and valid for up to six months. It
also directs learners to prepare on vehicle controls, road signs, and traffic rules.

## Permanent licence timing

Delhi public guidance says a valid learner licence must be held for at least 30 days before the
competence test, and the permanent licence application should be made within six months of
learner-licence issue.

## Appointments and fees

Delhi public guidance identifies appointment and fee payment as driving-licence service topics.
Current availability, fee amounts, and any transaction must be checked in the approved public
source; DigiLicense does not carry out that transaction.

## Renewal and replacement

Delhi public guidance lists renewal and duplicate-licence requirements separately. This corpus is
reference material only and does not determine an applicant's eligibility or submit a request.
